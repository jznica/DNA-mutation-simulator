Model assumptions and limits
  • Dose model. Hits per site follow a Poisson process with rate λ = μ₀ + k·C·t, so the chance a site changes is 1 − e−λ. EMS dose–response is close to linear at the low doses used in practice.
  • Calibration of k. k = 4.5×10⁻⁶ per G:C pair per (% v/v · h), set so 0.3% EMS for 12 h gives roughly one mutation per 170 kb across a 36% GC genome, the order of density reported for EMS-mutagenised Arabidopsis. Treat it as an order-of-magnitude figure, not a lab constant.
  • Spectrum. EMS changes are G:C→A:T transitions only (over 99% in published EMS spectra). Background replication errors use μ₀ = 7×10⁻⁹ per bp per generation (the Arabidopsis spontaneous rate) with a 2:1 transition:transversion ratio.
  • The animation. The violet particles are illustrative: their number scales with concentration, and only particles that land on a mutated pair are drawn hitting it. Outcomes come from the probability model, not from particle collisions.
  • Not modelled. DNA repair saturation, cell death and sterility at high dose, sequence-context hotspots, insertions and deletions, strand bias.
  • Reading frame. Translation starts at position 1 with the standard genetic code. A trailing partial codon is shown but not translated.
